# Sample student guide (made up for this demo, not metropolia's real rules)

## Thesis supervision
Every thesis student is assigned a supervisor within three weeks of the thesis topic being approved. The student and the supervisor meet at least once a month. The student books the meetings and sends a short progress note to the supervisor two days before each meeting.

## Thesis topic approval
The student submits a one-page topic proposal to the thesis coordinator by the first day of the semester. The coordinator confirms or rejects the topic within 10 working days. A rejected proposal can be resubmitted once during the same semester.

## Thesis length and format
A bachelor's thesis is 40 to 60 pages, not counting appendices. The student must use the thesis template, which is available on the learning platform. References follow the numbered citation style.

## Use of AI tools in coursework and theses
Students may use AI tools for language checking, brainstorming and explaining concepts, if they add a short disclosure statement describing the tools and how they were used. AI tools must not be used to produce analysis results or text that is presented as the student's own work. Undisclosed use of AI tools is treated as academic misconduct.

## Plagiarism check
Every thesis is run through a similarity check before the examination. If the similarity is above 20 percent, the examiner reviews the report and may ask the student to revise the thesis.

## Course enrolment
Enrolment opens four weeks before the semester starts and closes at the end of the first week of teaching. Late enrolment needs the approval of the programme coordinator and must be requested before the end of the second week.

## Cancelling a course enrolment
A student can cancel an enrolment without penalty until the end of the second week of teaching. After that, the course is marked as failed unless the student has a documented reason such as illness.

## Exam registration
The student registers for an exam at least seven days before the exam date. Late registration is not possible. Students who fail an exam can register for the retake exam in the retake period at the end of each semester.

## Absence from an exam because of illness
A student who is ill on the exam day sends a medical certificate to the study office within five working days. The certificate allows the student to take the exam in the next retake period without it counting as a failed attempt.

## Practical training
Practical training is worth 30 credits. The training agreement must be signed by the student, the employer and the training coordinator before the training starts. The student submits a training report within four weeks after the training ends.

## Recognition of prior learning
A student can ask to have earlier studies or work experience recognised as credits. The student submits an application with certificates and course descriptions to the study office. The decision is made within 15 working days.

## Leave of absence
A student can apply for a leave of absence of up to two semesters for health reasons, parental leave or military service. The application must be submitted before the semester begins.

## Study guidance
Study counsellors meet students by appointment, which is booked in the guidance portal. A drop-in session is open on Wednesdays from 12 to 14. Counsellors help with study plans, delays in studies and choosing courses.

## Grade appeals
A student can ask for the written reasons for a grade within 14 days after the grade is published. A request for a rectification must be submitted to the examiner within 14 days after receiving the reasons.

## IT support and accounts
Forgotten passwords are reset in the self-service portal. The IT helpdesk is open on weekdays from 8 to 16 and helps with accounts, Wi-Fi and software licences.

## Course feedback
The course feedback survey opens in the last week of each course. The results are shared with the students at the start of the next study period, together with the actions the teachers plan to take.
